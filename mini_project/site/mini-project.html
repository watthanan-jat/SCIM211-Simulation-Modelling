Minimum Model

What every café simulation must contain

01

Operating period

A 120-minute arrival period, with accepted customers allowed to finish after closing.

02

Random demand

Random arrivals and both simple and complex drink orders.

03

Random service

Service times that depend on order type and use the supplied seed.

04

Resources

A configurable number of identical baristas.

05

Queue

FCFS service initially, with a configurable maximum queue and recorded lost customers.

06

Event logic

Arrival, service-start, and service-completion logic with a consistent simulation clock.

07

Economics

Revenue, ingredient cost, scheduled staffing cost, and profit.

08

Outputs

Customer counts, waiting measures, throughput, utilisation, revenue, cost, and profit.

09

Replication

One result per supplied seed so uncertainty can be estimated.

10

Flexibility

Inputs must come from config; do not hard-code the baseline values.

The unseen challenge may change arrivals, staffing, service times, customer priority, queue admission, the menu, a promotion, an equipment condition, or the closing policy.

What the checker tests
  • The file executes and defines both required functions.
  • The official configuration works without editing the file.
  • Required results are numeric, finite, nonnegative where appropriate, and internally consistent.
  • Customer counts, order counts, revenue, cost, profit, and throughput balance.
  • The same seed reproduces the same result and different seeds produce stochastic variation.
  • Zero demand produces zero arrivals and service outputs.
  • Order-type probabilities and queue limits affect the model.
  • Additional baristas do not increase mean waiting in the stress scenario.
  • The model does not alter the supplied configuration dictionary.
  • The replication function returns one correct row per seed.

Week 16 Wednesday Lab

Two-hour hackathon format

Hour 1

Assigned challenge

  1. Adapt the prepared simulation.
  2. Compare the required policies.
  3. Use the instructor-supplied seeds.
  4. Run at least 40 replications per policy.
  5. Prepare one table, one graph, and a 95% confidence interval.
  6. Make a recommendation and identify one limitation.
Hour 2

Pitch and questions

Each group receives six minutes: four minutes to pitch and two minutes for questions.

  1. Student 1: model and assigned problem.
  2. Student 2: experiment and results.
  3. Student 3: recommendation and limitation.

Assessment

Marking guide

Prepared simulation · 4 marks

  • Entities, queue, resources, events, and random inputs: 1.5
  • Reusable and configurable implementation: 1.0
  • Replications, seeds, outputs, and uncertainty: 1.0
  • Verification, documentation, and assumptions: 0.5

Live challenge · 3 marks

  • Correct implementation of the assigned change: 1.0
  • Appropriate experiment and uncertainty: 1.0
  • Evidence-based recommendation and limitation: 1.0

Pitch communication · 1 mark

  • Clear four-minute explanation with an effective table or graph: 1.0

Individual understanding · 1 mark

  • Explains their contribution, code, modelling choice, result, and answer independently: 1.0
Readiness-check scoring rule

PASS is compulsory but earns no automatic marks. If the submitted file displays FAIL, the common-interface criterion receives 0. Other criteria are marked only from evidence that genuinely runs and can be explained.
